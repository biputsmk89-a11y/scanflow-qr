# Google Play Store Asset Specifications — ScanFlow QR

Guidelines and dimension requirements for store graphics and visual collateral.

---

## 1. Required Graphic Assets

| Asset Type | Dimension | Format | Max File Size | Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **App Icon** | 512 x 512 px | 32-bit PNG | 1 MB | Flat transparent or solid rounded squircle, no drop shadow |
| **Feature Graphic** | 1024 x 500 px | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | 15 MB | No transparency, showcases ScanFlow QR branding & tagline |
| **Phone Screenshots** | Min 1080 x 1920 px (16:9 or 9:16) | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | 8 MB each | Min 4, Max 8 screenshots showcasing core features |
| **7-inch Tablet Screenshots** | Min 1200 x 1920 px | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | 8 MB each | Min 1, Max 8 screenshots |
| **10-inch Tablet Screenshots** | Min 1920 x 1200 px | JPEG or 24-bit PNG | 8 MB each | Min 1, Max 8 screenshots |
