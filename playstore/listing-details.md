## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 Characters)
**ScanFlow QR: Scan. Create. Connect.**

Experience lightning-fast scanning, professional QR customization, and intelligent security verification — all in one modern, native Android app built with Material 3.

### 🔍 Lightning Fast Camera Scanner
- **Multi-Format Barcode Engine**: Powered by Google ML Kit, scan QR Codes, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, and Code 93.
- **Scan from Photos**: Scan barcodes directly from your device gallery.
- **Smart Tools**: Flashlight toggle, camera flip, tactile haptic feedback, and duplicate scan prevention.

### 🛡️ Smart Security & Phishing Protection
- **Link Verification**: Automatically analyzes URLs to verify HTTPS encryption, flags unencrypted links, direct IP addresses, and suspicious domains.
- **Zero-Auto-Execute**: Never opens dangerous links or executes payloads without your explicit confirmation.
- **App Lock & Biometrics**: Protect your scan history and custom QR codes with Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or 4-digit PIN.

### 🎨 Pro QR Code Studio & Customizer
- **11 Supported QR Types**: Create QR codes for Websites, Wi-Fi Networks, Contacts (vCard), Phone Numbers, SMS, Emails, Geo Locations, Calendar Events, Payments (UPI/Crypto/PayPal), and Social Media.
- **Deep Visual Styling**: Customize foreground and background colors, dot pattern styles (Square, Rounded, Dots, Diamond), and corner eye shapes (Square, Rounded, Circle).
- **High-Res Export & Share**: Save crisp PNG images directly to your gallery or share instantly via Android Sharesheet.

### 🗄️ Offline-First & Private
- **100% Local Storage**: All scan records and custom QR codes are saved locally on your device using Room SQLite.
- **Search & Filter**: Find any past scan in milliseconds with live search and category filters.
- **Favorites & Bulk Actions**: Pin important codes, export data, and manage your collection with one tap.
